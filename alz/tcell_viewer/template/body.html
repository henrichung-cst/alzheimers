Glossary

Backbone
A receiver-specific Receptor → EM → Target route. It summarizes the receiver-side signaling route shared across one or more sender ligands.
Receiver
The cell type whose receptor, EM, and target genes define the intracellular side of the modeled pathway.
Sender
The cell type providing the ligand side of a sender-to-receiver pathway.
TPDS
Transcript-level pathway differential score: modeled disease-associated change in log signaling probability. Positive and negative signs indicate opposite directions of modeled signaling change.
Passed both nulls
For a specific backbone and contrast, both permutation checks passed: kinase enrichment and receiver-specific wiring.
Passing contrasts
Count of genotype-by-timepoint contrasts, out of 9, where the backbone passed both nulls. A value of 3 means three contrasts passed, not three null models.
NES
Normalized Enrichment Score from bulk kinase MEA on ForPerseus log2FC.
FDR
False discovery rate used to threshold kinase MEA significance.
n_sig
Count of contrasts where FDR < the active threshold.
Pathway support
Backbone-level provenance derived from full pathways: expression-confirmed, kinase-imputed, or mixed when both appear across contributing paths or contrasts.
Support
Kinase contribution to a pathway backbone edge; larger absolute values indicate stronger phosphoproteomic support for that modeled route.
Observed score
Permutation-tested pathway score used for backbone-level additivity and graph edge summaries.
Contrast
Day vs d2 baseline. Kinase MEA contrasts are donor1-only (no IMAC for donor2); Incytr pair-mode contrasts vary per donor. Active vocabulary is set by the donor toggle.
