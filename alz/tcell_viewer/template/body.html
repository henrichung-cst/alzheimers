Cell-state nomenclature

Evidence-backed per-cell states, used consistently across donor scRNA, attribution, kinase-explorer, and pathway (Incytr) views. Lineage (CD4/CD8) is assigned from CITE-seq surface protein; each cell then gets one functional state scored from marker panels (see the labeling-evidence report). Colors: CD4 in oranges, CD8 in blues, pale → dark along the naive → exhausted progression.

CD8+ T-cells

CD8
Bare-lineage CD8 cell eligible for no named state (annotated by lineage alone).
CD8NaiveLike
Naive stemness (TCF7, LEF1) and homing (CCR7, SELL), without cytotoxic, activation, or exhaustion programs.
CD8RestingMemory
Resting/memory identity (IL7R, CD27), without acute activation or exhaustion.
CD8ActivatedEffector
Acute activation (CD69, IL2RA, TNFRSF4, ICOS, CD40LG) plus effector function (GZMB, PRF1, IFNG, TNF), without exhaustion.
CD8CytotoxicEffector
Granzyme program (GZMB, GZMH, GNLY) plus perforin (PRF1), without the exhaustion transcription-factor program.
CD8Exhausted
Late exhaustion signature (HAVCR2, LAG3, ENTPD1, TOX, NR4A1) positive and exceeding the acute-activation and effector-function scores.
