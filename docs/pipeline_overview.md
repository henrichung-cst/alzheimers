# Pipeline Overview

An end-to-end account of how the 72-animal Song cohort's TMT phosphoproteomics becomes a ranked set of candidate intercellular signaling pathways with kinase and cell-type evidence attached.

## Executive summary

The pipeline takes in 72 TMT total-proteome channels and their matched IMAC phosphoproteomics from the Song cohort, paired snRNA-seq on 28 of the 72 animals, and two external brain atlases. It produces, on the bulk side, a per-kinase hypothesis table that pairs MEA enrichment scores with transcriptomic cell-type attribution and confidence tiers; and, on the integration side, 22 receiver-indexed Parquet files scoring 462 sender-receiver ligand → receptor → effector → target pathway chains across nine disease-by-timepoint contrasts, together with cross-pair aggregates (backbone recurrence, hub matrix, target convergence) and per-contrast dual-null permutation statistics. The framing throughout is hypothesis-generating: the outputs are convergent functional evidence for candidate mechanisms, not mechanistic pathway validation.

## 1. Cohort and measurements

The Song cohort is a 4 genotype × 3 timepoint × 2 sex factorial with 3 biological replicates per group, giving 72 animals. Genotypes are WT, APP knock-in (App), T22 tauopathy (Tau), and the double knock-in (T22/APP, here called ApTt). Timepoints are 2, 4, and 6 months. All 72 animals are run through both TMT total-proteome quantification (6 plexes × 10 channels + bridge) and IMAC-enriched phosphoproteomics. A 28-animal subset — selected so that each genotype × timepoint cell has paired snRNA-seq — contributes ~63,000 nuclei (Allen Cell Type Mapper annotations, 22 of 24 SEA-AD subclasses represented). Two external references are used only to anchor cell-type interpretation, never as direct evidence streams: the SEA-AD human MTG postmortem transcriptomic effect sizes (stratified by continuous pathology score into early-/low-CPS, late-/high-CPS, and full strata) and the Whole Mouse Brain 10Xv3 expression matrix (per-subclass kinase specificity against the SEA-AD-mapped taxonomy).

The primary analysis is **males only** — 33 animals after outlier exclusion. Sex × genotype interactions are not identifiable at 3 replicates per factorial cell, so the primary path runs males and keeps the female subset for sensitivity. The **full-cohort** mode re-fits the same model across all 72 animals, treating sex as a main-effect nuisance covariate.

## 2. Stage 1 — Data ingestion

Ingestion (`data_ingest.py`) maps raw TMT channels to animals across the 6 plexes × 10 channels + bridge layout, assembles the sample manifest, and performs three QC passes. First, phosphosites are linked to their parent proteins by UniProt accession plus residue-level matching, achieving 91.7% site-to-parent matching over 86.5% of parent proteins — both coverage numbers end up stored on the site table so downstream stages can filter on them. Second, per-plex and cross-plex PCA surfaces batch structure, genotype separation, and candidate technical outliers. Third, a robust within-group z-score procedure flags individual animals whose phospho intensity profile sits far enough from its genotype × timepoint × sex group median to be excluded from downstream OLS. The outlier list is what drives the 36-male-animals-before-QC → 33 after. The same list is available for, but does not automatically apply to, the full-cohort analysis. The canonical diagnostic is the PCA + z-score composite at [`../outputs/reports/data_ingest/pca_plots/outlier_diagnostic.png`](../outputs/reports/data_ingest/pca_plots/outlier_diagnostic.png).

## 3. Stage 2 — IRS normalization and stoichiometry correction

Cross-plex normalization uses Internal Reference Scaling against the bridge channel — the same normalized object is always produced over all 72 samples, because sample filtering happens downstream at OLS time, not here. Stoichiometry correction then computes, per site per sample, `stoichiometry = log2(phospho) − log2(parent protein)`. The subtraction removes the largest confounder in bulk phospho readouts: a site that rises in disease because its parent protein got upregulated no longer scores as a phosphorylation change. Conversely, a site where the *fraction* of phosphorylated protein has shifted still shows up.

Empirically, the subtraction matters. Roughly 379 of the kinases whose raw phospho signal looks significant turn out to be abundance-driven (their ranking collapses after correction), 101 retain both activity and abundance signal, and about 12 kinases that are invisible in raw phospho emerge as activity-driven only after correction. Those 12 are the kinases the pipeline is best-positioned to say something new about, and they are why the correction is a required stage rather than an optional preprocessing flag.

## 4. Stage 3 — Kinase enrichment (MEA)

`kinase_attribution.py --enrich` fits a site-level OLS against the stoichiometry matrix using a 7-parameter main-effects factorial design (`const, App, Tau, Int, female, time_4mo, time_6mo`) across the qualifying animals — 33 in males-only, 72 in full-cohort. `App`, `Tau`, and `Int` are genotype indicators with the coding `WT=(0,0,0), APP=(1,0,0), T22=(0,1,0), T22/APP=(1,1,1)`; the `Int` column captures the synergistic excess of the double knock-in over the sum of its components, not an interaction with sex or timepoint. Sex and timepoint enter as main effects and act as nuisance covariates — a deliberate choice, because at 3 replicates per factorial cell a full-interaction design would consume degrees of freedom that provide little benefit for the downstream GSEA-based enrichment, which aggregates across thousands of sites for each kinase.

Three contrasts come out of the coefficient vector: the **App** main effect (`β_App`), the **Tau** main effect (`β_Tau`), and the **ApTt** full effect (`β_App + β_Tau + β_Int`). Contrast variances use `Var(c'β) = c'(X'X)⁻¹c · σ²` with two-tailed t-tests and Benjamini-Hochberg correction per contrast. The contrast β values are median-centered across sites and winsorized at the 1st/99th percentile before feeding GSEA pre-ranked enrichment against the kinase-library motif-substrate catalog. Output: per kinase per contrast, a normalized enrichment score (NES) and FDR. Kinases with FDR < 0.25 on at least one contrast move forward. The OLS-vs-naive diagnostic at [`../outputs/reports/kinase_attribution/diagnostic_naive_vs_ols.png`](../outputs/reports/kinase_attribution/diagnostic_naive_vs_ols.png) confirms that the OLS framing materially reshapes the ranking versus a per-group-mean baseline — which matters because the factorial design was what the experiment was set up to exploit.

## 5. Stage 4 — Unified cell-type attribution

A kinase being active in bulk tissue does not, on its own, tell us which cell type is driving the signal. Bulk phosphoproteomics is tissue-averaged, so cell-type assignment in this pipeline is done through transcriptomic evidence rather than by inverting the bulk signal directly. Three evidence sources combine into a single attribution:

- **Within-cohort Song snRNA-seq concordance** (weight 3×). A per-gene, per-subclass OLS on the 28-animal snRNA-seq produces contrast-matched gene-level LFCs. For each kinase, concordance is `sign(NES_kinase) × median(per-gene LFC)` aggregated over the kinase's substrate genes — if the kinase is enriched in the "up" direction and its substrates' mRNA is also going up in that contrast in a given cell type, concordance is positive. Same animals, same contrasts, same disease model; this is the densest cross-modal evidence available in the cohort.
- **Cross-species SEA-AD concordance** (weight 1×). Human MTG effect sizes provide a disease-anchored check independent of the mouse model. SEA-AD is pathway-matched per contrast: App contrasts use the early/low-CPS stratum, Tau contrasts use the late/high-CPS stratum, and ApTt uses the full stratum.
- **WMB expression specificity**. Per-subclass kinase-gene expression from the Whole Mouse Brain atlas. The gate is specificity ≥ 2× the uniform baseline (2/24 across the 24 SEA-AD subclasses). This is a necessary but not sufficient condition: a kinase needs to be present in a cell type for attribution there to be biologically plausible, but presence alone doesn't imply activity.

The three combine into an `effective_concordance` scalar and a `combined_score`. Attributions with `effective_concordance ≤ 0` are rejected outright. The surviving attributions are tiered by confidence:

- **High** — WMB specificity gate passes, and positive concordance comes from at least one of Song or SEA-AD. If both contribute positively, the tier is still High (the three_way evidence_basis value).
- **Moderate** — either one source contributing on its own, or SEA-AD concordance without Song support. SEA-AD-only attributions are capped at Moderate regardless of magnitude: a human-postmortem signal without within-cohort corroboration cannot reach High in this framework.
- **Low** — marginal cases that pass the concordance sign gate but miss the WMB specificity bar or carry only weak evidence (for example, mouse expression specificity alone without either concordance source positive).

The `evidence_basis` column records exactly which sources contributed per row, with values `three_way, within_cohort, cross_species, mouse_expression_only, song_only, human_concordance_only`, and `weak`. The taxonomy is 24 SEA-AD subclasses; 22 of those are represented in the Song snRNA-seq subset and are therefore the ones that can earn Song-backed High attributions. An illustrative per-subclass view (microglia, top 50 kinases by evidence strength) is at [`../outputs/reports/attribution_recovery/bubble_plots/bubble_microglia_top50.png`](../outputs/reports/attribution_recovery/bubble_plots/bubble_microglia_top50.png).

## 6. Stage 5 — Attribution recovery and final tables

`attribution_recovery.py` runs cross-contrast consistency analysis — does a kinase's attribution in the App contrast behave coherently with its attribution in Tau and ApTt — and writes trajectory labels (for example, App-specific, shared, ApTt-divergent). It then assembles four canonical tables:

- **`kinase_hypothesis_table.csv`** — the primary deliverable. One row per (kinase, contrast) with attributed cell types, confidence tier, evidence basis, and cross-contrast trajectory. Reading a row: "kinase X, enriched in contrast C, is supported as acting in cell type Y at confidence tier T, on the basis of evidence sources E, with cross-contrast pattern P." This is the table biological interpretation starts from.
- **`kinase_activity_matrix.csv`** — the same information in wide form: one row per kinase, columns are NES and FDR for each of the three contrasts, plus the trajectory label.
- **`celltype_evidence_table.csv`** — one row per (kinase, cell type) with the static evidence that led to the attribution, WMB-gated. Useful when the question being asked is cell-type-first.
- **`celltype_kinase_profiles.csv`** — the cell-type-first NES profile (one row per cell type × kinase), for heatmap-style summaries.

A representative heatmap view for microglia is at [`../outputs/reports/attribution_recovery/bubble_plots/heatmap_microglia_top50.png`](../outputs/reports/attribution_recovery/bubble_plots/heatmap_microglia_top50.png). From the bulk side of the pipeline, Stage 5 is the terminal output; Stage 6 begins the hand-off to the integration layer.

## 7. Stage 6 — Incytr integration

The integration layer asks a different question than Stages 1–5. Given that we now know which kinases are active and where they are likely acting, which *cell-cell signaling pathways* does that kinase activity support? The pipeline uses Incytr for this. Incytr models each intercellular signaling pathway as a four-gene chain — **Ligand** (sender cell) → **Receptor** (receiver) → **Effector Molecule / EM** (receiver) → **Target** (receiver) — with valid chains enumerated from the curated IncytrDB catalog. Per-link Hill functions of joint node expression combine into a signaling probability `SigProb = Hill(L × R) × Hill(R × EM) × Hill(EM × T)`, and a logistic transform of SigProb produces TPDS (Transcriptomic Pathway Differential Score) in [−1, +1] — the condition-level change in co-expression along the chain. Additional evidence layers add to produce PDS, with `kinase_boost = PDS − TPDS` recording how much non-expression evidence moved the score per pathway.

The factorial mode uses per-animal SigProb and OLS contrast estimation rather than condition-level averaging. Across the 15 male snRNA-seq animals, a per-pathway 10-parameter OLS (`const, App, Tau, Int, time_4mo, time_6mo, App×time4, App×time6, Tau×time4, Tau×time6`) yields, for each of 9 disease×timepoint contrasts (App/Tau/ApTt × 2mo/4mo/6mo), a per-pathway TPDS, standard error, and p-value. (`df_resid = 5`, so the p-values are conservative and ApTt contrasts — driven by 1 animal per timepoint — have the widest SE.) This is a deliberately finer-grained design than the bulk MEA's 3-contrast model: the MEA layer treats sex and time as nuisance to maximize enrichment power; the integration layer interrogates them because temporal and genotype-combination dynamics are among the things it is trying to resolve.

Two architectural pieces are worth understanding at the lab-meeting level.

**Receiver-centric enumeration.** Three of the four pathway nodes — Receptor, EM, Target — are receiver-determined, and only the Ligand varies with sender. The orchestrator exploits this: it loops receivers on the outside, prunes the L2 (R→EM) and L3 (EM→T) edge lists once per receiver, then inner-loops senders, pruning L1 (L→R) edges and running a single DuckDB 3-way join with inline SigProb filtering at cutoff 0.01. Receiver-side scoring — expression lookups, per-animal SigProb, OLS contrast estimation — is computed once per receiver and broadcast across all 21 senders in a vectorized pass rather than rebuilt 21 times. Under a 12 GB systemd memory cap, this runs 462 pairs × 9 contrasts to completion, emitting 22 receiver-indexed Parquet files (`recv_{receiver}.parquet`) with `sender` as a column and per-contrast TPDS / SE / p-value columns.

**Dual kinase channels.** Incytr natively scores kinase genes that pass the 10% expression detection threshold when those kinases appear as pathway nodes. Most disease-relevant kinases don't pass: phospho activity can be high while mRNA is sparse or post-translationally regulated. The integration adds a second channel for them. For each pathway, the **external kinase support score** is

```
kinase_support_score = median( |NES_K| × IDF_G × attribution_weight_K )
```

over kinase→substrate edges where kinase K (disease-significant in MEA, FDR < 0.25 for the contrast) phosphorylates substrate gene G, where G is an EM or Target node of the pathway, and where K is *not* already a node at that position (to avoid double-counting the internal channel). `IDF_G = 1 / log(N)` with N the number of MEA-significant kinases targeting G, discounting hub substrates like Srrm2 (targeted by 80+ kinases) so that connections to promiscuous substrates count for less. `attribution_weight_K` pulls from Stage 4's unified attribution: the full `combined_score` when K is attributed to the pathway's receiver cell type, and 0.25× that score when K is attributed only to the sender (the sender discount reflects that intracellular kinases in the sender cell rarely phosphorylate substrates in the receiver). The median over edges is robust to both hub-substrate inflation and single-outlier dominance. A kinase-imputed expansion step admits receiver genes below the 10% expression detection threshold when they are substrates of high-FDR kinases (FDR < 0.10, tighter than the main gate) strongly attributed to the target receiver, gated by an R-side expression floor at `det_rates ≥ 0.05` and with a soft-rescue weight proportional to `1 − best_fdr` so that borderline-FDR kinases contribute proportionally less than strongly significant ones.

Outputs: 22 receiver Parquet files; per-pair `kinase_support_scores.csv`; per-pair `adjusted_rankings.csv` that re-rank pathways under `TPDS + λ × kinase_support_score` for λ in {0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0}; and per-pair `reranking_summary.json`. The hub heatmap at [`../code/integration/intermediates/factorial/all_pairs/aggregation/hub_heatmap_grid.png`](../code/integration/intermediates/factorial/all_pairs/aggregation/hub_heatmap_grid.png) shows how signaling load distributes across sender × receiver combinations and contrasts.

## 8. Stage 7 — Cross-pair aggregation and interpretation

After per-pair scoring, three cross-pair views summarize the 462-pair landscape:

- **Backbone recurrence.** For each receiver, Receptor × EM × Target triples that recur across many senders into that receiver. A backbone that shows up via most senders is more likely to reflect receiver-intrinsic biology than sender-specific signaling.
- **Hub matrix.** A 22 × 22 sender × receiver grid of aggregate signaling intensity per contrast. Rows and columns with dense high-magnitude entries identify cell-type hubs.
- **Target convergence.** Genes reached by many senders through many distinct routes — candidate points of convergent regulation.

Statistical support comes from a per-contrast dual-null permutation run at the backbone level. **Null 1 (enrichment null)** shuffles which MEA-significant kinases each edge draws from the full 311-kinase MEA universe, holding attribution weights at their median; it asks whether disease-significant kinases concentrate in specific backbones. **Null 2 (wiring null)** shuffles kinase identity and samples attribution weights *within each backbone's receiver cell type* (not globally, because cross-cell-type attribution variance inflates null scores for contrasts with few MEA-significant kinases); it asks whether the specific kinase-substrate-to-cell-type wiring is non-random given the receiver. Storey's q-value (fixed λ = 0.5, computed on the active-backbone set — those with at least one kinase-substrate edge) controls the FDR. Null 2 is the binding constraint in practice: most backbones that pass Null 1 (enrichment) fail Null 2 (wiring); the set passing both is the high-confidence layer. Null 2 significance varies strongly by receiver: GABAergic interneuron subclasses (Lamp5 Lhx6, Chandelier, Sst Chodl) carry the densest wiring signal, while excitatory glutamatergic types (L2/3 IT, L4 IT) and astrocytes are sparse.

`examine_factorial.py --run` produces the interpretation outputs from these aggregates: **additivity** — compares ApTt effect to `App + Tau` per backbone (sub-additivity, which we observe, is a biologically informative deviation from the null expectation that the double knock-in is the sum of its components); **temporal trajectories** — classifies each backbone's 2mo/4mo/6mo behavior per genotype (monotonic, peaked, late-onset, etc.); **cell-type centrality** — identifies sender and receiver cell types that carry disproportionate signaling load; **kinase concordance** — per-contrast summaries of how often kinase-substrate evidence agrees in sign with TPDS. ## What the pipeline does and doesn't claim

The outputs are convergent functional evidence for candidate intercellular signaling pathways, at per-contrast resolution across disease genotypes and timepoints. They are not mechanistic validation. Three caveats belong attached to any downstream interpretation:

1. **Shared-animal dependence.** The 28 snRNA-seq animals are a subset of the 72 TMT animals, so the Song within-cohort concordance that feeds Stage 4 attribution weights shares subjects with the phospho measurements it is used to attribute. The two evidence streams are different modalities (mRNA vs phosphoprotein), but they are not statistically independent. A SEA-AD + WMB-only run is implementable as the conservative sensitivity comparison — if kinase support rankings materially shift under that restriction, the Song contribution should be disclosed; if they don't, the dependence is immaterial.
2. **Unsigned external kinase support score.** The external channel is magnitude-only by design, to preserve the biology of inhibitory phosphorylation (a kinase activated, pathway downregulated — an inhibitory phospho-mediated mechanism — would otherwise be erased by a signed score). The unsigned score conflates concordant and discordant pathway hits in the top-ranked set. Any downstream aggregation or top-N summary needs to stratify by the concordance flag that each pathway carries; otherwise it mixes biologically opposite signals under the same rank.
3. **IncytrDB constrains discoverable biology.** Enumeration is bounded to four-gene chains in IncytrDB's curated catalog. Non-canonical interactions, recently discovered signaling modes, and architectures that are not four-gene (longer, shorter, branching) are invisible to the pipeline. The framework prioritizes convergent evidence within known templates, it does not discover novel signaling architectures.

## How to run it

```bash
pixi run live                                                  # bulk: ingest → normalize → enrich → attribute → recover
bash code/integration/run_factorial_all_pairs.sh               # 462 pairs × 9 contrasts
bash code/integration/run_factorial_permutations.sh            # per-contrast dual-null backbone tests
python code/integration/adapters/examine_factorial.py --run    # additivity, temporal, cell-type, kinase interpretation
```

Environment variables that matter at run time: `ANALYSIS_MODE` (`males_only` default or `full_cohort`), `PAIR_FILTER` (e.g. `"Microglia-PVM:L5 IT"` or `"*:L5 IT"` to restrict), `ENABLE_KINASE_IMPUTATION` (default on), and `MEMORY_LIMIT_GB` (R memory guard, default 10, runs under a 12 GB systemd scope).
